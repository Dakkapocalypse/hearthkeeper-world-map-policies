Hearthkeeper World Map Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

The Hearthkeeper community administrators operate this Discord Activity.

What we use and why

  • Discord account and access. When you sign in, the Activity uses your Discord user ID, display name or username, and server membership and roles to identify you and check which features you can use.
  • Game accounts and positions. Hearthkeeper uses linked gaming account identifiers and server-reported character names, online status, and in-game coordinates to match characters, show map pins, and deliver actions. Map visibility depends on the game, your sharing preferences, and the viewer’s access.
  • Actions and credits. Hearthkeeper keeps records of requests and their outcomes, including the requester, target, and credit transactions where applicable. We use these records to apply game actions, manage balances, prevent duplicate charges, and investigate problems.
  • Requests and support. The relay uses network addresses for short-lived request limits. When you submit a support ticket, staff receive the information you include so they can investigate and respond.

Who can see information

Discord handles sign-in and provides identity and membership data. The public connection runs through Tailscale and forwards Activity requests to the host. Authorized viewers can see shared character pins and markers. Community administrators can see connected characters in their private Manager and review records needed for support and moderation. Your credit balance is shown privately to you and can be reviewed by authorized staff.

How long information is kept

The host keeps Activity sign-in sessions in memory for at most 15 minutes. Account links, sharing preferences, action history, credit records and support tickets can persist beyond a session. Staff review requests to remove records alongside the need to retain them for security, moderation, account history, or credit accounting.

Your choices and requests

Use Players → Share my location to control sharing where the map provides that option. Some games use their own visibility settings. To request access, correction, unlinking, or deletion of your Hearthkeeper data, use /ticket report privately in the community Discord or contact a community administrator. Staff may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request.

See also the World Map Terms of Service and Discord Privacy Policy.
