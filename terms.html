Hearthkeeper World Map Terms of Service

Effective November 13, 2026

Using the World Map

Hearthkeeper World Map is a community-run Discord Activity. It shows maps and server information and lets eligible members request game actions. You must follow Discord’s terms, the game’s rules, and the rules of the community and server you use. Access to features depends on your server membership, roles, game availability, and, where applicable, player participation settings.

Fair use

Use the map and game actions respectfully. Do not use location information to harass players, impersonate another player, evade access controls, or abuse game actions, credits, or cooldowns. Community staff may restrict access or remove content that breaks these rules.

Actions and availability

Map information can be delayed. Game actions depend on live server checks and may fail or be unavailable. Review the action, target, and credit cost before confirming. Hearthkeeper charges credits after the game reports successful delivery or application. If the result is unclear, the credits may stay on hold while staff review it. Features, maps, and availability may change as the community and games change.

Contact

Use /ticket report with Hearthkeeper in the community Discord, or contact a community administrator if you cannot use the command. See the Privacy Policy for information about your data.
